{# The testimonials page: the theme's section layout, plus everything needed to fill the list that the `testimonials` component leaves empty. The card and the two kinds of paragraph are markup rather than strings in the script, so that a quote reaches the page as text and never as markup — the list may one day be served from somewhere that collects the quotes rather than from this repository. One icon per network is here for the same reason the rest is: the feather SVG is written once, in `social_icon`. `data-network` is the set of networks a quote may name — the whole set, and the same for all of them. A quote naming anything else gets no icon and no link rather than a broken profile URL. A network only links when the quote also carries a `handle`; without one the icon just says where it came from. A quote may also carry a `date` (an ISO day), an `url` that makes the name itself clickable, and an `image` — a face, shown as a circle beside the words. Not everyone wants to be published with one, so a quote without it simply keeps the full width of the card.
